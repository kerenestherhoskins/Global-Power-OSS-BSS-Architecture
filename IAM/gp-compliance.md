## Purpose
The GP Compliance department ensures regulatory adherence, federal contracting compliance, and internal audit readiness. This role reviews IAM policies, monitors system access, and maintains documentation for audits.



## Core Permissions
-Read-only access across all OSS and BSS systems for audit purposes
-Ability to generate compliance reports and review IAM policies
-Access to regulatory documentation and audit logs
-No write access to operational or billing systems

## Responsibilities
-Conduct internal audits an verify IAM policies adherence
-Review OSS/BSS workflows for regulatory compliance
-Maintain documentation for federal and state reporting
-Coordinate with GP Admin on access reviews an security updates
