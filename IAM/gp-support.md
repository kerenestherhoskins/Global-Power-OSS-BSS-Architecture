
## Purpose
The GP Support department handles customer service interactions, technical troubleshooting, and issue escalation. This role ensures customer satisfaction and resolves service-related problems.





## Core Permissions
- Access to trouble-ticket systems and customer support logs
- Read-only access to OSS monitoring dashboards
- Ability to escalate issues to GP Operations
- No access to billing or compliance systems




## Responsibilities
- Respond to customer service tickets and technical inquiries
- Escalate unresolved issues to GP Operations
- Maintain service logs and feedback reports
- Ensure customer satisfaction through timely resolution
