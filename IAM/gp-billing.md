## Purpose
The GP billing department manages invoicing, payment processing, account reconciliation, and financial reporting. This role ensures accurate billing and support revenue assurance.




## Core Permissions
-Read/write access to BSS billing and revenue assurance workflows
-Ability to generate invoices, process payments, and reconcile accounts
-Access to financial reporting dashboards
-No access to OSS operational systems

## Responsibilities
-Generate invoices and manage payment processing
-Reconcile accounts and report revenue metrics
-Collaborate with GP Compliance for audit readiness
-Maintain billing accuracy and prevent revenue leakage
