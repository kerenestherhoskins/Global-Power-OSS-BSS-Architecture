## Purpose
The GP Operations department manages day-to-day telecom operations, including provisioning, service activation, network inventory, and monitoring. This role ensures services are delivered, maintained and optimized.




## Core Permissions
-Read/write access to OSS workflows (provisioning, activation, inventory, monitoring)
-Ability to execute operational scripts and automation tools
-Access to networks inventory and service activation logs.
-Limited access to BSS order-management for provisioning aligmnt
